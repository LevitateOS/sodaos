# Not-found page illustration

Historical provenance: the 2026-09-10 replacements/corrections are recorded in
[story-art-prompts.md](story-art-prompts.md). Earlier prompts and acceptance below
refer to earlier image bytes; the [story review](../../../docs/illustration-story-review.md)

A small wayfinding scene softens an otherwise sparse 404 card while preserving the native status code, custom/default explanation, conditional recovery URL and version display. A map avoids implying deletion: Forgejo also uses this page for inaccessible resources. Decorative empty alt text keeps the image out of the error announcement.

One robot, map and compass only on a thin cobalt felt mat. Fine matte paper grain and folded seams, soft warm studio lighting, slightly elevated three-quarter view, cream/navy/cobalt/mint palette. Compact centered silhouette, complete objects with generous margins, readable at 160px wide. No text, letters, numbers including 404, logos, screens, laptop, plants or drink. Landscape 1536x1024 PNG with transparent background.
