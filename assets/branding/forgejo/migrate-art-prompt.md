Historical provenance: the 2026-09-10 replacements/corrections are recorded in

No extra props, plant, drink, laptop, provider logo, words, letters, numbers or success checkmark. Slightly elevated three-quarter view, warm soft studio light, cream/navy/cobalt/mint palette. Compact scene on a thin curved cobalt felt mat, complete objects and comfortable margins, readable at 180px wide. Landscape 1536x1024 PNG with transparent background.
