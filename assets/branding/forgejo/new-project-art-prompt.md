## Generation prompt

Create a Soda papercraft illustration for creating a new project board. Image 1 is the primary robot identity, paper material and light reference; image 2 controls compactness; image 3 controls proportions. Same short cream matte cardstock robot, rounded rectangular head, navy inset face, cyan oval eyes and small smile, cobalt circular earcaps, navy mittens/joints/soles. Robot on left places a single mint blank task card onto a small upright cream folded-paper planning board on right. Board has three simple cobalt vertical dividers and just two other blank cream cards, not a screen. This is starting a plan, not a completed workflow: no checkmarks, charts, trophies or flags. Fine paper grain and visible seams, matte materials, thin cobalt felt mat, warm soft studio light, slightly elevated three-quarter view. Compact balanced scene readable at 180px. No plants, drinks, laptop, text, letters, logos, watermarks or extra robots. Entire scene centered with generous margin, 1536x1024 landscape PNG with actual transparent alpha outside objects and mat; no background, no painted checkerboard. Preserve reference robot identity.
