## Transparency and rejected candidates

Several initial outputs painted a checkerboard instead of providing alpha. Those
were passed back to the built-in tool for background removal while preserving the
scene; none was repaired through programmatic raster editing. Acceptance requires
RGBA PNG, transparent outer corners, visible subject coverage and actual browser
compositing on both cream and navy surfaces. A raw viewer can display hidden RGB
colors beneath transparent pixels; the composited review is authoritative.

The first Wiki scene was rejected after the thumbnail comparison because its
triangular book silhouette repeated New repository. The selected revision uses a
suspended U-shaped book hammock. The first Pull requests background-removal output
was rejected because it still lacked alpha; the accepted retry used the original
identity-correction output as its reference, with this exact prompt:

Remove the background from this image. Make the background transparent. Keep the robots, all objects, and blue mat exactly as they are. Output an RGBA PNG with an alpha channel.
