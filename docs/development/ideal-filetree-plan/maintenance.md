# Maintaining source ownership and coverage

## Keeping the plan current after every merge

Reconcile a changed responsibility with its existing package, placement and
desired-tree owner when the change affects those contracts. A merge with no such
delta needs no ceremonial plan refresh, body audit or bookkeeping commit.

Use the [remaining-outcome catalog](../factory-remaining-tasks.md) for execution.
Filetree R02 owns missing source-to-duty coverage; library/native consumers and
complexity work retain their existing outcome IDs. Do not create a parallel queue.

For an affected leaf, update its real owner/destination and changed large-file
concern. Check affected references and boundary assertions proportionately. Keep
historical inventories, intervals and receipts pinned to their actual source;
a path-count refresh is not body or installed proof. State remaining limits in
the existing coverage index without regenerating unchanged evidence.

Preserve recorded language and product decisions. Proposed packages and parked
seams are not implementation authorization. Revisit accepted scopes only for
changed inputs, a concrete failure or a missing criterion.
