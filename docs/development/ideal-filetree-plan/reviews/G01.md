## Intended model

Fountain owns browser login, session and persistent native page/panel lifetime. Soda receives verified ephemeral actor/contribution/generation context and applies current product policy through a private bridge. It owns local product data, not native session truth. Actors are current Forgejo user, configured Soda operator, Fountain host and Soda extension/dashboard. Existing Spaces/global and Workspace/panel contributions admit ordinary actors; Tailnet/admin is operator scoped. Public browser IDs and generation labels do not authorize effects. Browser callbacks and background factory service grants have separate lifetimes. No second login, cookie, broad proxy or session ledger is proposed.
